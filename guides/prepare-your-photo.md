# Prepare Your Photo

The quality of the poster depends heavily on the reference photo you attach to Google Flow, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other image generator.

## Checklist

- **Resolution:** at least 1500 px on the long side.
- **Sharp focus:** the logo, label and edges must be readable.
- **Even lighting:** avoid harsh shadows and strong color casts.
- **Whole product visible:** do not cut off parts you want shown.
- **Simple background:** easier for the AI to isolate the product. A plain wall or table is enough.
- **Straight angle:** front or slightly above, unless the style needs a dynamic angle.
- **One product per photo:** for several products, send separate photos and describe the arrangement in `[EXTRA REQUEST]`.

## Tips

- Shoot near a window with soft daylight, product on a neutral surface.
- Clean the product and remove reflections from glossy packaging.
- Take two or three angles (front, 3/4, detail of the label) and attach them all if your tool allows multiple reference images.
- For services (no physical product), use a photo of the place, the team or a result.
- In Google Flow, add the photo as an ingredient/reference. In ChatGPT and Gemini, upload it in the same message as the prompt.

## Extra instruction when the product keeps getting altered

```
Do not modify the product in any way. Keep its exact shape, colors, label and logo as in the attached photo.
Only change the background, lighting and surrounding elements.
```

> **Reminder:** always replace everything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own preferences before sending a prompt.
