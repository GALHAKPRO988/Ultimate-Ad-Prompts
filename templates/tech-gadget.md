# Template: Tech & Gadget

## When to use
Electronics, apps, devices, software, accessories.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Modern tech product ad.
- Dark or deep-gradient background (navy, graphite, violet) with soft rim lighting.
- Product floating or on a glossy surface with realistic reflections.
- Subtle light trails, glow or abstract geometric shapes; keep it tasteful.
- Clean geometric sans-serif typography, key spec or benefit highlighted in an accent color.
- Maximum 3 short feature callouts.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Wireless earbuds
- Headline: Silence, upgraded
- Subheadline: 40 h battery · Active noise cancelling
- CTA: Available now
- Tone: Sleek, confident
- Format: Landscape 16:9
