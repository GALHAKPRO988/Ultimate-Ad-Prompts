# Template: Minimalist Product

## When to use
Cosmetics, coffee, accessories, packaged goods, anything where the product should be the hero.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Minimalist premium product ad.
- Clean, solid or very subtle gradient background in a single soft color.
- Soft studio lighting, gentle realistic shadow under the product.
- Lots of negative space. Product centered or on the rule of thirds.
- Small, elegant sans-serif text. Headline in light or medium weight.
- No clutter, no decorative icons, no more than 2 colors plus neutrals.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Single-origin coffee, 250 g
- Headline: Wake up properly
- CTA: Order today
- Tone: Calm, premium
- Format: Vertical 3:4
