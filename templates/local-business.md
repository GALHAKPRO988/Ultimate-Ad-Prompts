# Template: Local Business

## When to use
Barbershops, gyms, repair shops, clinics, cleaners, small shops announcing a service or opening.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Friendly local-business poster.
- Bright, welcoming palette with 2 brand colors and plenty of contrast.
- Photo of the product, service or storefront as the main visual, cleanly cut out or framed.
- Big, simple headline stating the benefit; one short supporting line.
- Contact block always visible: address, phone, opening hours, social handle.
- Optional discount or "Grand opening" ribbon.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Neighborhood bike repair shop
- Headline: Back on two wheels
- Offer: Free safety check this month
- CTA: Drop by or call
- Tone: Friendly, trustworthy
- Format: Vertical 3:4
