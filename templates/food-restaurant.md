# Template: Food & Restaurant

## When to use
Dishes, drinks, menus, bakeries, delivery promotions.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Appetizing food advertisement.
- Warm, natural lighting that makes the food look fresh and juicy.
- Rich, warm color palette (reds, oranges, creams) with a textured surface (wood, stone, linen).
- Close, slightly high-angle framing; subtle steam, droplets or ingredients around the dish.
- Friendly serif or hand-lettered headline, readable sans-serif for details.
- Price or offer in a rounded badge.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Smash burger combo
- Headline: Juicy. Stacked. Yours.
- Offer: Combo for 9.90
- CTA: Order on our app
- Tone: Fun, mouth-watering
- Format: Story 9:16
