# Template: Real Estate

## When to use
Properties for sale or rent, new developments, interior design, agencies.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Real estate listing poster.
- The property photo fills most of the poster, brightened and color-balanced, with straight verticals.
- A clean solid panel (white, navy or beige) holding the text, with strong alignment.
- Key data in an icon-free line: bedrooms · bathrooms · m2 · location.
- Price large and unmistakable. Trustworthy sans-serif typography.
- Agency logo and contact in a footer band.
```

## Example brief
- Product: 3-bedroom apartment with terrace
- Headline: Your new home
- Subheadline: 3 bed · 2 bath · 110 m2 · City Center
- CTA: Book a viewing
- Tone: Trustworthy, bright
- Format: Vertical 3:4
