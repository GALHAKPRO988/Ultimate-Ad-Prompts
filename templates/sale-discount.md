# Template: Sale / Discount

## When to use
Seasonal sales, flash offers, Black Friday, clearance, limited-time promotions.

## Style block
```
STYLE: High-impact promotional poster.
- Bold, saturated background (red, yellow, electric blue or black with neon accents).
- Huge discount figure (e.g. "-30%") as the dominant typographic element.
- Product slightly angled with dynamic shadow or burst shapes behind it.
- Heavy, condensed sans-serif type. Strong contrast.
- A clear urgency element: "Limited time" or an end date in a badge.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Running shoes
- Headline: Summer sale
- Offer: -30% until Sunday
- CTA: Shop now
- Tone: Urgent, energetic
- Format: Square 1:1
