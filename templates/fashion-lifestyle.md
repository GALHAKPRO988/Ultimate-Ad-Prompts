# Template: Fashion & Lifestyle

## When to use
Clothing, jewelry, bags, sportswear, lifestyle brands.

## Style block
```
STYLE: Editorial fashion campaign.
- Magazine-like layout with asymmetric composition and generous margins.
- Natural or golden-hour lighting, subtle film grain, muted or earthy palette.
- Product shown in context (on a surface, in a scene) without altering it.
- Elegant high-contrast serif headline, tiny uppercase sans-serif for secondary text.
- Brand name or logo small and refined in a corner.
```

## Example brief
- Product: Leather tote bag
- Headline: Carry the day
- CTA: New collection online
- Tone: Elegant, understated
- Format: Vertical 3:4
