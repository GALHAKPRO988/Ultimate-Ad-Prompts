# Mega-Prompt: Tech & Gadget Poster

**When to use:** Electronics, apps, devices, software and accessories.

**How to use it:** attach your product photo as a reference image in your AI generator (Google Flow, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, Firefly...), copy the whole block below, replace every `[BRACKET]` with your own preferences, and generate.

## Copy & paste this prompt

```
You are a world-class advertising art director, commercial photographer and graphic designer. Create ONE finished, professional advertising poster using the attached reference photo of my product as the hero of the design.

=== 1. REFERENCE PHOTO RULES (MOST IMPORTANT) ===
- The attached photo shows my real product: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE, e.g. wireless noise-cancelling earbuds with charging case]. Treat it as the single source of truth for how the product looks.
- Keep the product 100% faithful to the photo: exact shape, proportions, colors, materials, textures, labels, packaging, logo and every printed detail.
- Do NOT redesign, distort, stretch, simplify, recolor or "improve" the product itself. Do NOT add parts, buttons, ports, flavors, variants or accessories that are not in the photo.
- Do NOT invent, replace or add any brand names, logos, certifications, awards or watermarks other than the ones that already appear on the product or in my brief.
- You MAY: cut the product out of its original background, correct perspective and exposure, clean dust or reflections, relight it realistically, scale it, rotate it slightly for the composition, and place it in a brand-new scene.
- The product must look physically real: correct contact shadows, believable reflections and consistent lighting with the new scene.
- If the photo shows a service, a place or a person at work, keep them recognizable and natural, with correct anatomy and no distorted faces or hands.

=== 2. POSTER BRIEF ===
- Brand / business name: [BRAND NAME]
- Product or service: [PRODUCT OR SERVICE, e.g. wireless noise-cancelling earbuds with charging case]
- Main goal of the poster: [GOAL: sell / announce an offer / promote an event / attract customers / launch a product]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE: age range, interests, city or country]
- Brand tone of voice: [TONE: e.g. sleek, confident, premium, innovative]
- Main colors: [COLOR 1], [COLOR 2], accent [COLOR 3] (or write "extract the palette from my photo")
- Language of ALL text on the poster: [LANGUAGE]
- Aspect ratio / format: [ASPECT RATIO: 3:4 vertical / 1:1 square / 9:16 story / 16:9 landscape / A3 print]
- Where it will be used: [USE: Instagram feed / story / shop window / flyer / web banner / print]

=== 3. EXACT TEXT TO RENDER (spell every word perfectly, character by character) ===
- Headline (max 6 words): "[HEADLINE]"
- Subheadline or key offer: "[SUBHEADLINE OR OFFER]"
- Extra short line (optional): "[EXTRA TEXT]"
- Call to action: "[CALL TO ACTION]"
- Contact details: "[WEBSITE / PHONE / ADDRESS / SOCIAL HANDLE]"
Do not add any other words, slogans, fake prices, fake dates, lorem ipsum or gibberish text anywhere in the image. If a text field above is left empty, do not render it and do not invent a replacement.

=== 4. STYLE & CONCEPT ===
Premium tech product launch visual in the style of flagship electronics keynotes and product pages: sleek, futuristic and precise. It should communicate performance and quality at a glance.

=== 5. BACKGROUND & SCENE ===
A deep dark or rich gradient environment in [COLOR 1, e.g. midnight navy, graphite, violet], with subtle abstract elements: soft light trails, glowing rings, faint grid lines, particles or glass-like shapes related to [FEATURE, e.g. sound waves, speed, battery, AI].

=== 6. PROPS & SUPPORTING ELEMENTS ===
Product floating or resting on a glossy reflective surface with realistic reflections and micro details. Optional exploded-view hints or floating components only if they stay faithful to the real product.

=== 7. LIGHTING ===
Dramatic rim lighting in [ACCENT COLOR], soft top light, specular highlights along the edges, subtle glow and bloom, crisp reflections on glass and metal, high dynamic range.

=== 8. CAMERA & PERSPECTIVE ===
Hero three-quarter angle, 85-100 mm look with controlled perspective, ultra-sharp product, slight depth blur on background elements, dramatic sense of scale.

=== 9. COLOR PALETTE ===
Dark base with one vivid accent ([ACCENT COLOR, e.g. electric blue, magenta, lime]) used for highlights, key numbers and the call to action; neutral tones for text.
Keep colors consistent with my brand colors from the brief. Make sure the product stays the most visually attractive and colorful element, with strong separation from the background.

=== 10. TYPOGRAPHY ===
Clean geometric or grotesque sans-serif, tight and modern; headline in medium or bold weight, key spec numbers oversized in the accent color, labels in small uppercase with wide tracking.
Use at most two typefaces. Text must be crisp, perfectly legible, correctly spelled, and free of warping, doubled letters or random symbols. Maintain high contrast between text and its background (place text on calm, uncluttered or darkened/lightened areas). Keep clear space around text.

=== 11. COMPOSITION & LAYOUT ===
Product dominant at the center or on the right, headline on the left in a clear block, up to three short feature callouts with thin connector lines, price or release date highlighted, call to action in a button style at the bottom, legal micro-text only if I provide it.
General composition rules: strong single focal point on the product (it should occupy roughly 40-60% of the poster unless the style says otherwise); clear visual hierarchy (headline > offer > call to action > contact details); balanced use of negative space; respect safe margins of about 6% from every edge; align elements to a clean grid; guide the eye in a natural path from the product to the headline to the call to action.

=== 12. MOOD & ATMOSPHERE ===
Innovative, confident, premium, high-performance, cutting-edge.

=== 13. EXTRA DETAILS ===
Specs to show (only these, no invented numbers): [SPEC 1], [SPEC 2], [SPEC 3].
Optional extra details from me: [ANY EXTRA REQUEST, e.g. add a small logo in the top-left corner, leave the bottom 20% empty for adding my own text, include a QR code area].

=== 14. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS ===
Final output: one single image, [ASPECT RATIO], ultra high resolution, razor-sharp detail on the product, clean edges without halos or cut-out artifacts, professional color grading, realistic materials, print-ready quality, no borders, no frames, no watermarks, no UI elements, no mockup of a phone or a wall (just the flat poster artwork itself).

=== 15. NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS (AVOID ALL OF THIS) ===
Avoid: a modified or redesigned product, wrong or invented logos, misspelled or garbled text, extra text I did not request, blurry or low-resolution output, distorted hands or faces, extra fingers, duplicated products, cluttered composition, low contrast text, over-saturated or muddy colors, harsh artificial shadows, cheap stock-photo look, visible AI artifacts, watermarks, signatures, cropped-off product, cartoonish elements, cheap neon gamer clutter, fake spec numbers, wrong ports or buttons, distorted proportions, invented logos, low-contrast gray-on-gray text.

=== 16. DELIVERY ===
Generate the final poster now. If any bracketed field above was left unclear, make the most reasonable professional assumption and mention it in one short line after the image. Then offer two alternative variations: one with a different layout and one with a different color palette, keeping the product and the text identical.
```

## Follow-up prompts (copy & paste what you need)

Pick the line you need and paste it as a follow-up message in the same chat or project:

```
FIX THE TEXT: Regenerate the exact same poster, keeping the composition, colors, lighting and product identical, but render this exact text and nothing else: headline "[EXACT HEADLINE]", offer "[EXACT OFFER]", call to action "[EXACT CTA]", contact "[EXACT CONTACT]". Spell every word correctly, with no extra characters.

PROTECT THE PRODUCT: The product changed compared with my reference photo. Regenerate the poster with the product exactly as in the attached photo: same shape, colors, label and logo. Do not modify the product in any way. Only change the background, lighting and surrounding elements.

MORE LEGIBILITY: Keep everything the same but increase the contrast between the text and its background, enlarge the headline by [PERCENT, e.g. 20]%, and give the text more clear space. Place the text on a calmer area of the image.

CLEANER LAYOUT: Simplify the poster. Remove decorative elements, increase negative space, keep only the product, headline, offer and call to action, and align everything to a clean grid.

NEW FORMAT: Recreate this exact poster in [NEW ASPECT RATIO, e.g. 9:16 for stories], adapting the layout so the text and the product stay within safe margins, with the same style, colors and text.

NEW PALETTE: Keep the exact same layout, product and text, but change the color palette to [NEW COLORS, e.g. deep green and gold, pastel pink and cream].

MORE PREMIUM: Make the poster look more premium and expensive: refine the lighting, reduce visual noise, use more elegant typography and more subtle details.

MORE IMPACT: Make the poster more eye-catching: bolder contrast, larger headline, more dynamic angle for the product, stronger lighting, while keeping the text correct and the product faithful.
```

> **IMPORTANT NOTE:** always replace everything inside `[BRACKETS]` with your own preferences before sending the prompt. Every `[BRACKET]` is a placeholder (brand, product, headline, colors, contact details, format...). If a line does not apply to you, delete it. Never send the prompt with the brackets still in it.
